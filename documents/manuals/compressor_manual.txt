# Technical Manual: Rotary Screw Industrial Air Compressor (Model: SCR-CMP-620)

## 1. Operating Envelope
- Capacity: 25 m³/min FAD (Free Air Delivery)
- Maximum Discharge Pressure: 8.5 - 12.5 bar
- Normal Airend Discharge Temperature: 78°C - 92°C (Warning: 98°C, Emergency Trip: 105°C)
- Ambient Temperature Range: 5°C to 45°C
- Main Drive Motor: 110 kW TEFC Induction motor, 2960 RPM direct coupled

## 2. Rotor & Bearing Geometry
- Male Rotor: 5-lobe profile, forged steel with ceramic anti-friction coating
- Female Rotor: 6-flute profile, high-strength spheroidal graphite cast iron
- Bearing arrangement: Roller bearings at intake, paired angular contact bearings at discharge
- Clearance: Inter-lobe clearance 0.04 - 0.06 mm; End-face discharge clearance 0.035 mm

## 3. Servicing & Component Replacement
### Oil Separator Element Replacement
- Service life: 4,000 hours or when pressure drop across separator exceeds 0.8 bar.
- Residual oil content: Must remain below 3 ppm w/w in compressed discharge air.

### Minimum Pressure Check Valve (MPV)
- Inspect valve disc and spring every 8,000 operating hours.
- Set crack open pressure at 4.2 bar gauge to preserve internal lubrication pressure before system builds header pressure.

### Thermostatic Bypass Valve
- Opens flow to oil cooler when synthetic fluid exceeds 71°C. If valve sticks closed, rapid thermal runaway occurs within 15 minutes of full-load operation.

## 4. Error Codes & Telemetry Signals
- ERR-CMP-01: High airend discharge temperature (Trip at 105°C).
- ERR-CMP-02: Oil separator high differential pressure (> 1.0 bar).
- ERR-CMP-03: Excessive airend vibration velocity (> 6.5 mm/s RMS).
