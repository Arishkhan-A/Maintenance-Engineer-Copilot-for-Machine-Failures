# Technical Manual: 6-Axis Articulated Industrial Robotic Arm (Model: ROB-ARM-700)

## 1. Specifications & Payload
- Nominal Payload Capacity: 50 kg
- Reach: 2,050 mm
- Repeatability: ± 0.03 mm
- Servomotor Bus Voltage: 560V DC
- Axis J1-J3 Max Speed: 180°/sec; Axis J4-J6 Max Speed: 320°/sec
- Nominal Motor Temperature: 45°C - 70°C (Thermal alarm: 80°C)
- Permissible Harmonic Drive / Gearbox Vibration: < 1.5 mm/s RMS

## 2. Joint & Drive Train Architecture
- Harmonic Drive Reducers on wrist axes (J4, J5, J6)
- Cycloidal Precision Reducers on main base axes (J1, J2, J3)
- Absolute Optical Encoders (24-bit multi-turn resolution)
- Fail-safe electromagnetic disc brakes on all 6 axes

## 3. Maintenance Protocols & Overhaul
### Cycloidal Gearbox Lubrication
- Lubricant: Molywhite RE No. 00 synthetic grease.
- Replacement interval: Every 5,000 operational hours or 12 months.
- Grease replenishment pressure: Maximum 0.3 bar to prevent blowing lip seals.

### Axis Backlash Calibration
- Measure angular backlash using dial indicator mounted on robot faceplate.
- Re-tune servo gain parameters if backlash exceeds 0.08 arc-minutes.

### Cable Harness & Flex Dress Pack
- Inspect robot dress pack for outer polyurethane sheath chafing every 1,000 hours.
- Perform high-pot insulation resistance test (> 50 Megaohms at 500V DC).

## 4. Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC)
- ERR-ROB-01: Joint 2/3 servomotor thermal overload (> 85°C).
- ERR-ROB-02: Cycloidal gearbox excessive vibration / mechanical backlash surge (> 2.8 mm/s).
- ERR-ROB-03: Brake release solenoid current fault.
