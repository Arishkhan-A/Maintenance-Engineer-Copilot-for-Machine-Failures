# Technical Manual: Industrial Multistage Hydraulic Pump (Model: HYD-PMP-800)

## 1. Operating Parameters
- Maximum Flow Rate: 120 m³/h
- Working Pressure Range: 18 - 42 bar (Differential pressure threshold: 5 bar deviation)
- Permissible Fluid Temperature: 15°C - 75°C (Alarm at 78°C, shutdown at 85°C)
- Operating Speed: 1450 - 1780 RPM
- Mechanical Seal Flush Rate: Plan 11 or Plan 32 with clean barrier fluid at 2 bar above suction pressure

## 2. Critical Assemblies & Tolerances
- Impeller Clearance: Radial runout within 0.05 mm
- Mechanical Seals: Tungsten carbide vs. silicon carbide primary faces
- Wear Rings: Replace when diametrical clearance exceeds 0.45 mm
- Thrust Bearings: Angular contact duplex ball bearings 7314-BEP

## 3. Maintenance Protocols
### Cavitation Prevention & NPSH Verification
- If discharge pressure oscillates with high-frequency noise resembling gravel pumping, inspect suction strainer delta-P.
- Clean suction strainer if differential pressure exceeds 0.8 bar.

### Mechanical Seal Overhaul
- Inspect seal face flatness using monochromatic light. Max permissible waviness: 2 helium light bands (0.6 microns).
- Replace O-rings with fluoroelastomer (Viton) seals rated for 150°C.

### Oil Reservoir Management
- Lubricant: ISO VG 46 mineral hydraulic fluid.
- Filter micron rating: Beta 1000 >= 10 micron.
- Particle count limit: ISO 4406 cleanliness code 17/15/12 max.

## 4. Error Codes & Diagnostic Rules
- ERR-PMP-01: Low suction head / Cavitation risk. Suction pressure < 1.2 bar absolute.
- ERR-PMP-02: Excessive casing vibration. Axial RMS velocity > 6.0 mm/s. Indicates misalignment or bent shaft.
- ERR-PMP-03: Seal barrier pressure loss. Barrier fluid < suction pressure + 1.5 bar.
