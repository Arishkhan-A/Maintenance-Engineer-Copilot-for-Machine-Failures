# Troubleshooting Guide: Hydraulic Circuitry, Valves & Pressure Dynamics

## 1. Hydraulic Operational Envelope
- Normal working pressure: 20 - 35 bar (dependent on plant circuit).
- Fluid cleanliness: ISO 4406 cleanliness code 17/15/12 max.
- Maximum bulk fluid temperature: 65°C (Alarm at 70°C, emergency trip at 78°C).

## 2. Root Cause Analysis for Hydraulic Failures

### Pressure Loss & Severe Pulsation
- Root Cause A: Bladder accumulator nitrogen loss. When gas charge leaks, hydraulic shock pulses damage proportional valves.
  - Action: Measure gas pre-charge pressure with accumulator gauge; recharge dry nitrogen to 0.65 of minimum system pressure.
- Root Cause B: Pump suction cavitation due to clogged suction strainer or high fluid viscosity.
  - Action: Inspect suction differential pressure gauge; clean mesh filter; verify oil heater at cold startup.

### Excessive System Heat & Thermal Overrun
- Root Cause: Relief valve stuck cracked open or proportional directional valve internal bypass spool wear.
  - Action: Perform thermographic infrared scan of valve manifolds; any valve hotter than surrounding circuit indicates internal high-pressure bypass leakage.
