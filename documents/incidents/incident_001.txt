# Historical Incident Report: INC-2024-089
Date: 2024-03-14
Machine ID: M17 (CNC Milling Machine / Heavy Drive)
Severity: Critical (Catastrophic Spindle Bearing Seizure)
Downtime: 18.5 hours
Total Production Loss: $42,000

## 1. Sequence of Events
- 04:30 AM: Continuous telemetry recorded gradual vibration rise from baseline 1.8 mm/s to 4.2 mm/s over 3 shifts.
- 07:15 AM: Stator temperature rose gradually from 58°C to 88°C under static 65% spindle load.
- 09:40 AM: High-frequency demodulated accelerometer detected bearing cage rattling; alarm logged as medium priority.
- 11:12 AM: Drive DE bearing suffered micro-welding due to grease starvation and thermal runout, seizing the drive spindle and tripping thermal breaker.

## 2. Root Cause
- Drive-end 6312-C3 ball bearing underwent lubrication starvation because the automatic grease dispenser tube was crimped during prior tooling bracket changeover.
- Metal-to-metal contact led to exponential friction heat, raceway micro-spalling, and catastrophic seizure.

## 3. Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA)
- Replacement of bearing 6312-C3 and spindle shaft balancing.
- Rule established: When vibration velocity exceeds 4.5 mm/s accompanied by temperature rise > 15°C over baseline, immediate shutdown and manual grease inspection is MANDATORY.
- Spare part requirement: Bearing 6312-C3 (Qty: 2), Viton Seal ring (Qty: 2).
