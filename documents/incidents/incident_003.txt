# Historical Incident Report: INC-2024-178
Date: 2024-08-09
Machine ID: M12 (Rotary Screw Compressor Unit 3)
Severity: Critical (Thermal Overheat Trip & Airend Rotor Thermal Binding)
Downtime: 22.0 hours
Total Production Loss: $54,000

## 1. Sequence of Events
- 08:20 AM: Airend discharge temperature climbed from nominal 82°C to 96°C over 40 minutes under full delivery load.
- 08:50 AM: Oil cooler fan current was normal, but differential oil temperature remained negligible (< 3°C delta-T across heat exchanger).
- 09:05 AM: Unit executed emergency trip on ERR-CMP-01 (106.5°C logged).
- Thermal expansion caused male and female rotors to touch end-faces lightly, scoring rotor coating.

## 2. Root Cause
- Thermostatic bypass valve wax element failed and stuck in bypass position, circulating hot oil directly back into the compression chamber without passing through the radiator cooler core.

## 3. Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA)
- Replaced thermostatic bypass valve cartridge (Part #CMP-THV-620) and flushed lubricant circuit with synthetic coolant.
- Added temperature-differential monitoring rule: If discharge temp exceeds 92°C and delta across cooler is under 8°C, flag immediate thermostatic valve fault.
- Spare part requirement: Thermostatic Valve Kit CMP-THV-620, Coolant ISO VG 46 (50 Liters).
